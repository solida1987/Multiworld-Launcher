# Multiworld Launcher 2.9.49

## The waypoint KNOWN ISSUE note, rewritten

The note on the game's Overview page used to blame Diablo II 1.10 and send
players to Jerhyn in Act 2. Player reports showed the real shape of the
problem: every act tab except Act 1 drops out of the waypoint menu at once,
and talking to *any* town NPC — one or two conversations — brings all of them
back. Jerhyn was never special; he was just the traditional pick.

The game side fixes this at the source as of v3.7.4 / EX-1.1.12: the menu's
staleness is refreshed automatically, no NPC required. The card now says so,
and keeps the talk-to-an-NPC advice as the fallback for anyone not yet
updated, or in case it ever still slips through.
