# Multiworld Launcher 2.9.50

## The launcher has a logo

It is on the executable now — taskbar, Explorer, Alt-Tab — and on the home
page above the getting-started steps.

The icon carries seven sizes from 16 to 256 pixels. The frames below 48 px are
cropped in on the skull and the orbs rather than scaled down whole, because the
outer flames stop being readable at that size and only cost the rest of the
design its share of a very small box.

## A game with no artwork now shows the logo

Until now a game with no thumbnail of its own got a lettered box. It gets the
launcher's logo instead, so an entry we have not drawn art for yet still looks
like part of the launcher. The same image is in place as the generic tile.

## About the logo

It is an **adapted work**: the ring of coloured spheres comes from the
Archipelago logo, and the skull and flames are the adaptation.
